Crossover Gate — the skeptic's instrument

A private framework predicts a structural crossover in neural entropy data clusters at a specific value. This is the gate that tries to kill that prediction: given a measured crossover and a null from surrogate data, does the clustering survive — or would any value fit noise just as well? A "signal" verdict is one the gate could not kill.

The estate's half of a research dyad. One side predicts (a framework: consciousness as κ-tuned self-reference, a crossover at a specific value). The other side measures (an engine: entropy at scale, on real data). This gate is the seam between them — it adjudicates the measurement against a surrogate null, so the number has to earn belief. We built the instrument to falsify our own favourite number.
